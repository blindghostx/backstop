Backstop Privacy Policy

This policy covers Backstop for iPhone. Effective October 29, 2026.

The short version

Backstop doesn't collect, store or sell any of your information. Messages you check are checked on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything Backstop knows lives only on your phone, in storage that only Backstop and its own extensions can read:

  • Messages you check. The text you paste or share is checked on your phone, and your recent checks are saved there so you can look back at them. You can delete any of them, or all of them, at any time.
  • Screenshots. When you pick or share a screenshot, its words are read on your phone using Apple's built-in text recognition. The picture isn't sent anywhere or saved by Backstop.
  • Your name and your people. Your first name, if you choose to give it, and the names and phone numbers of the people you add are saved on your phone so Backstop can greet you and address messages to them.
  • Your family phrase. It's kept in your iPhone's keychain, the same secure storage iOS uses for passwords.

Deleting Backstop deletes your saved checks, your name and your people. iOS can keep the family phrase in your iPhone's keychain after Backstop is deleted, so if you install Backstop again on the same iPhone, your old phrase may still be set. You can change it in Backstop under Settings › Family phrase.

Contacts

When you add someone from Contacts, Backstop uses Apple's contact picker. Backstop only sees the one person you choose, and never reads the rest of your contacts.

Texting your people

When you tap Ask, Backstop opens Apple's Messages app with a message already written for you. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send yourself, and the message goes through Messages like any other text. Backstop never sends anything on its own.

The scam text filter

If you turn on Backstop's filter in the Settings app, iOS shows Backstop the text of messages from numbers that aren't in your contacts, so it can move likely scams to your Junk folder. That check happens on your phone, the filter has no internet access, and it never sees messages from your contacts. Nothing from the filter is saved.

Reminders, widgets and Siri

Payment hold reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone. Widgets, the Control Center button and Siri actions use the same information stored on your phone and send nothing elsewhere.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics tools inside the app.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of information, because we don't have any.

Children

Backstop is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

A future version of Backstop may offer smarter checks that send the text of a message you choose to check to Backstop's server and to Anthropic's Claude AI to analyze it. If that happens, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is sent and how it's handled before that version is released, and the app will tell you before anything leaves your phone.
