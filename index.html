Backstop

When a text or call feels off, Backstop checks it on your iPhone and helps you bring in your family before any money moves.

For iPhone · Luke Turner

Scammers win by getting you alone and rushing you. Backstop is a pause button for that moment, and a way to get the people you trust back in the room.

What it does

  • Ask your people

    One tap writes a text to the family or friends you picked, with the message you saw. Nothing goes until you tap Send, and they don't need the app.

  • A family phrase

    Something only your family knows, for the call that says your grandchild is in trouble. A cloned voice can't know it.

  • Help on a call

    Pick who the caller says they are, and Backstop gives you the words to say and tells you when to hang up.

  • A 24-hour payment hold

    Promise yourself a day before paying anyone who asked for money. Backstop reminds you when the day is up.

  • Checks on your phone

    Paste a text or pick a screenshot, and Backstop points out the warning signs in plain words. The check runs on your iPhone.

  • A scam text filter

    It can move clear scam texts from numbers you don't know into the Junk folder in Messages, without using the internet.
